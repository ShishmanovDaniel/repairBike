How drop-off works

No surprise bills. You approve the price before any work begins.

  1. Drop it off

    Book a slot online or just bring it in. We tag your bike and write down what you've noticed.

  2. We text a quote

    A mechanic inspects it the same day and texts you a price for labor and parts. You say yes or no.

  3. Ride it home

    We text when it's ready, usually within three days. Every repair comes with a free 30-day adjustment.

Questions

How long will my repair take?

Most repairs are done within three days. Flat fixes are usually done while you wait. If we need to order parts, we'll tell you the date when we send your quote.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. Labor prices stay the same. We just can't guarantee parts we didn't supply.

What if I don't approve the quote?

Pick up your bike at no charge. The inspection is always free.

Do you store bikes?

We hold finished bikes for 14 days. After that there's a $5 a day storage fee.

About us

Three mechanics, one garage door, and a rule that nobody gets a surprise bill.

How we started

Loose Spoke started in a one-car garage in 2016, fixing bikes for neighbors on weekends. People kept coming back because we explained what was wrong and only fixed what needed fixing.

Today we're on Mill Street with three repair stands, but the rule hasn't changed: we inspect first, text you a price, and don't start until you say yes.

  • Free inspection on every bike, even if you don't go ahead.
  • Price before work. The quote you approve is the price you pay.
  • 30-day adjustment. If something shifts after a repair, we fix it free.
